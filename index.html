About Ihya Socials
"Your Islamic brand exists for a reason. My work is to make sure the world can find it."

Ihya Socials is a social media management service built specifically for Muslim-led product brands. The name Ihya — إحياء — means revival, and that is exactly what this service is designed to do: bring your brand's online presence back to life.

Many Islamic product brands have extraordinary products — beautifully crafted Mushafs, thoughtfully designed prayer mats, stationery built around the rhythms of Islamic life — but their Instagram presence doesn't reflect that quality. The content is inconsistent, the captions are rushed, and the strategy is non-existent. That gap is what Ihya Socials exists to close.

As an English major with a deep love for reading, writing, and the Muslim lifestyle space, I bring both professional content skills and genuine cultural understanding to every brand I work with. I don't need to research what resonates with your audience — I am your audience.

Ihya Socials operates on a simple belief: a brand built with halal intention deserves halal marketing — content that is honest, modest, values-aligned, and genuinely valuable to the people it serves.

The Approach
Strategy built around the Islamic calendar

Unlike general SMMs, Ihya Socials plans your content around Ramadan, Eid, Dhul Hijjah, and the full Islamic calendar — turning every sacred season into a meaningful growth opportunity for your brand.

The Difference
Cultural authenticity you can't outsource

A general SMM can learn social media strategy. They cannot learn what it means to build a brand for the Muslim community, for the sake of Allah, in a non-Muslim majority country. That understanding comes from lived experience — and it shows in every piece of content.

Non-Negotiables

Every piece of content — every caption, every visual — is created within the boundaries of Islamic values. No exceptions, no compromises.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to provide my own photos?

Yes — you provide raw photos and videos of your products. I handle editing, design, captions, and scheduling. I will give you a simple shot list each month so you know exactly what to capture.

How do I send you my content?

Via a shared Google Drive folder or WhatsApp — whichever you prefer. I make the process as simple as possible for you.

Will the content be reviewed before posting?

Always. Every post is sent to you for approval before it goes live. Nothing is published without your sign-off.

Do you work with brands outside UK, US, and Canada?

Currently I specialise in English-language content for brands based in the UK, US, and Canada. If you're based elsewhere but sell to English-speaking markets, get in touch to discuss.

Is there a minimum contract length?

Packages are month-to-month with no long-term contract required. I believe results should earn your continued trust — not a contract.

How are payments made?

Via PayPal, Wise, or Payoneer — whichever works best for you. Payment is due at the start of each month before work begins.
